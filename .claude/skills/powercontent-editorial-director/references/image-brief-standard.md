# Image Brief Standard

## Default Style

Use text-free premium 3D animation unless the user asks otherwise. For Naver SA thumbnails, defer to the `sa-thumbnail-creative-director` skill for current guide assumptions, 640 x 640 master production, 214 x 214 crop safety, SERP visual analysis, and prompt wording.

The style should be:

- polished and editorial;
- not childish;
- not photorealistic unless requested;
- not flat icon-like;
- warm but not cluttered;
- suitable for small 214x214 search ad thumbnails.

## Required Image Set

For a full Power Content package, produce:

- one hero thumbnail;
- three body images;
- one bottom CTA banner concept.

Each image brief must include:

- scene description;
- what to avoid;
- composition;
- color and lighting;
- people and objects;
- no text, numbers, or logo confirmation;
- what must differ from previous month images.

## Product Fit

Scene must match the insurance product:

- Driver insurance: road, intersection, school zone, accident response, legal document review, safe driving context.
- Home fire insurance: kitchen leak, ceiling stain, electrical outlet, balcony, storm preparation, home facility check.
- Golf or hole-in-one: golf course, tee-off, green, club, scorecard, golf bag.
- Cancer or health insurance: medical consultation, checkup result, family discussion, treatment plan, health records.
- Dental insurance: dental chair, tooth model, consultation, checkup.
- Travel insurance: airport, luggage, passport, flight board, travel preparation.
- Student/long stay overseas: campus, dorm, long-stay luggage, documents.
- Event liability: venue, audience flow, safety fence, staff checklist.
- Pregnancy/birth: maternity notebook, baby room, ultrasound, family preparation.

If the asset pool lacks enough matching images, do not borrow unrelated product images. Mark the slot as new generation required and provide a clear generation brief.

## Avoid

- text overlays;
- brand logos;
- exact competitor visual copying;
- the same character, pose, and background repeated across four slots;
- product-irrelevant reference assets;
- disaster scenes that feel sensational or frightening.

## CTA Banner

The bottom banner should be a scene, not a text layout. Describe a wide composition that can later receive separate copy outside the image if needed. Keep the generated image itself text-free.
