# SA Linkage Contract

Power Content and SA material should share one monthly thesis. Do not write a long article and then generate unrelated search ads.

## Mapping

- Article reader question -> SA title.
- Article decision criteria -> SA description.
- Secondary article check -> SA additional description.
- Official next action -> SA promotion.
- Article sections -> SA sitelinks.
- Article image thesis -> thumbnail concept.

## Rules

- Additional description must not repeat the main description.
- Promotion must not promise a benefit; it should guide the user to a next step.
- Sitelinks should be information architecture, not slogans.
- If the article angle is seasonal, SA copy must use the same selected month and event validity.
- If the article angle is SERP whitespace, SA copy should name the under-explained question without copying competitors.

## Example

Power Content thesis:

`운전자보험 검색 뒤 형사합의금과 변호사선임비용을 함께 보는 이유`

SA linkage:

- Title: `형사합의금 조건 읽기`
- Description: `운전자보험 약관에서 비용 항목과 적용 조건을 나눠 보세요.`
- Additional description: `변호사선임비용은 시점과 제외 조건까지 함께 읽어보세요.`
- Promotion: `약관 항목 확인`
- Sitelinks: `비용항목`, `약관확인`, `보험료`, `가입안내`
