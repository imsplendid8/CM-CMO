# Editorial Standard

## Editorial Position

Power Content should start from a real reader question, not from a product sales angle. A strong piece helps the reader decide what to check next:

- `운전자보험을 검색한 사람이 형사합의금과 변호사선임비용을 왜 함께 보는가`
- `주택화재보험 검색자가 누수와 풍수재를 헷갈리는 지점은 어디인가`
- `해외여행보험 검색자가 출국 전 가격보다 먼저 확인해야 할 조건은 무엇인가`

Avoid vague hooks. If the first paragraph could fit every insurance product, rewrite it.

## Article Shape

Use this editorial arc for publishable drafts:

1. Reader problem: name the concrete situation and search intent.
2. SERP reality: identify what the search page repeats and what it fails to explain.
3. Decision criteria: break down the actual checks a reader should make.
4. Product/rider translation: connect terms or riders to everyday scenes without promising outcomes.
5. Seasonal relevance: use only if it is valid for the selected month.
6. Checklist: turn the article into a practical pre-application review list.
7. Official CTA: point to official product information, premium calculation, and terms review.

## Title Patterns

Prefer specific, question-led, or process-led titles:

- `운전자보험 검색 뒤 형사합의금을 먼저 보는 이유`
- `주택화재보험에서 누수와 풍수재를 따로 읽어야 하는 순간`
- `해외여행보험, 출국일보다 먼저 확인할 세 가지`
- `골프보험에서 홀인원보다 배상책임을 함께 보는 이유`

Avoid interchangeable titles:

- `OO보험 필요한 순간`
- `OO보험 꼼꼼히 알아보기`
- `OO보험으로 든든하게 준비`
- `OO보험 보장내용 확인하기`

## Body Rules

- Use short, concrete Korean sentences.
- Explain one idea per paragraph.
- Use comparison rows or small lists when the reader must distinguish conditions.
- Treat product terms as raw material for reader judgment, not as sales bullets.
- Do not inflate ordinary checks into anxiety.
- Do not overuse `보장`, `담보`, `대비`, `안심`, `든든`.

## Output Fields

For each direction, include:

- title
- main keyword
- supporting keywords
- search intent
- SERP differentiation
- cannibalization risk
- recommendation status
- why this month

For the final concept, include:

- reader question
- editorial thesis
- section outline
- article draft
- review-sensitive phrases
- CTA
