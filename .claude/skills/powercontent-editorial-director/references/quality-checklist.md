# Quality Checklist

Before final delivery, run this check and report only meaningful issues:

## Strategic Fit

- The selected month matches every seasonal claim.
- Direct Home, TM, auto insurance, and unrelated business lines are excluded.
- The main topic is not only a product-name swap of a previous topic.
- The reader problem is visible in the title and first paragraph.

## Editorial Quality

- The piece explains what to check, not merely that insurance is important.
- The body has concrete scenes, decision criteria, and comparison points.
- The article can stand alone as useful content before the CTA.
- The final CTA is official-information oriented.
- The final tone has passed an anti-AI-tell review without changing facts or review-sensitive meaning.

## SERP Fit

- SERP table stakes are acknowledged but not copied.
- Saturated competitor language is avoided.
- Whitespace is used as the content angle.
- Monthly or annual variation is explicit.
- SERP evidence comes from structured text when available, not screenshot impression alone.

## Copy Safety

- No guaranteed coverage, claim payment, eligibility, price, or review outcome.
- Review-sensitive expressions are marked.
- `갑작스러운`, `든든`, `꼼꼼`, `보장하세요`, and empty `대비` formulas are absent or justified.

## Keyword And Cannibalization

- Supporting keywords are relevant to the product and reader task.
- Autocomplete modifiers are used only when they fit the product.
- Past title/body overlap is checked.
- High-risk cannibalization has a proposed alternative.
- SA linkage preserves the same thesis instead of creating unrelated search ad copy.

## Visual Quality

- Every visual scene matches the product.
- The four image concepts do not repeat the same person, pose, background, and object.
- Text, numbers, and logos are excluded from the image itself.
- If source assets are repeated, the output says new generation is required.
