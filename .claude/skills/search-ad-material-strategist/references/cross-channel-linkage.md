# Cross-Channel Linkage

SA, Power Content, and image thumbnails should share one monthly message axis.

## From Power Content To SA

Translate the article thesis into search material:

- Reader question -> title.
- Decision criteria -> description.
- Secondary check -> additional description.
- Navigation or official action -> promotion.
- Article sections -> sitelinks.

Example:

- Power Content thesis: `운전자보험 검색 뒤 형사합의금과 변호사선임비용을 함께 보는 이유`
- SA title: `형사합의금 조건 읽기`
- Description: `운전자보험 약관에서 비용 항목과 적용 조건을 나눠 보세요.`
- Additional description: `변호사선임비용은 시점과 제외 조건까지 함께 읽어보세요.`
- Sitelinks: `비용항목`, `약관확인`, `보험료`, `가입안내`

## From SA To Image

Image briefs should visualize the same decision moment without text overlays.

- Search action -> phone or laptop comparing official screens.
- Selection criteria -> checklist or side-by-side documents.
- Terms navigation -> policy booklet, highlighted section shape, documents.
- Seasonal scene -> real monthly situation.
- SERP whitespace -> under-explained detail in a concrete life scene.

Do not use a generic calculator image for every slot when product-specific visuals are available. When thumbnail quality matters, apply the `sa-thumbnail-creative-director` standard: 640 x 640 square master when upload-ready, 214 x 214 crop safety, no text overlays, premium 3D animation, product-fit scenes, and monthly variation.
