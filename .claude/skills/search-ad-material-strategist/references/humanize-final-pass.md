# Humanize Final Pass

Use a humanize-style pass only after strategy, safety, and length checks are complete.

## Preserve

- product names;
- legal or policy terms;
- rider names;
- figures;
- dates;
- source labels;
- review-sensitive meaning.

## Check

- Are sentence endings too uniform?
- Is `확인해 보세요` repeated too often?
- Are noun-heavy report phrases making the copy stiff?
- Does the copy sound like an internal report instead of an operating material?
- Did the rewrite make any claim stronger than the draft?

## Edit Direction

Prefer small changes:

- change one repeated ending;
- replace a vague noun phrase with a concrete action;
- remove filler such as `보다`, `꼼꼼히`, `든든하게`;
- keep short search-ad rhythm.

Do not perform broad stylistic rewrites if they risk changing meaning. For long editorial text, use the separate `humanize-korean` skill when explicitly requested.
