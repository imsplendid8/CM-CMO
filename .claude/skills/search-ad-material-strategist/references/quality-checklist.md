# Quality Checklist

Before final delivery, check:

## Data Basis

- SERP basis is text-structured, not only screenshot impression.
- Capture date, device, and keyword are visible or assumptions are labeled.
- Monthly diff is used when available.
- News context is not used as fear pressure.

## Material Quality

- Five sets are meaningfully different.
- Description and additional description do not repeat the title.
- Promotion is not a benefit promise.
- Sitelinks are usable navigation labels.
- Power Content and image linkage share the same axis.

## Safety

- No absolute superiority, guaranteed coverage, guaranteed payment, guaranteed eligibility, or unverified channel claim.
- Review-needed wording is marked.
- Direct Home, TM, auto insurance, and unrelated lines are excluded.

## Anti-AI-Tell

- No repeated `확인해 보세요` endings across most candidates.
- No generic `갑작스러운`, `든든`, `꼼꼼`, `대비하세요` formulas.
- No product-name-swapped repetition.
- Copy reads like compact operating material, not a padded report.
