# Naver Material Limits

Use the repository's current Naver material guide when available. If no repository data is available, apply conservative defaults and label them as assumptions.

## Conservative Working Limits

- Ad title: keep short, usually within 15 Korean characters when producing compact search material.
- Description: keep within 45 Korean characters unless a supplied guide says otherwise.
- Additional title: max 15 Korean characters.
- Additional description: max 45 Korean characters.
- Promotion text: max 14 Korean characters.
- Sitelink name: max 6 Korean characters.
- Sitelink set: 3-4 links when creating a complete set.

## Batch-Level Checks

Do not only validate row-by-row. Check ad group-level distinct counts when preparing export-ready material:

- Additional titles should stay within the allowed group count.
- Additional descriptions should stay within the allowed group count.
- Promotion texts should stay within the allowed group count.
- Sitelink bundles should stay within the allowed group count.

If limits are uncertain, say the output is a review draft, not an upload-ready Naver file.
