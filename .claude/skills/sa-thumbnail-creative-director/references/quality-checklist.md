# Quality Checklist

Before final delivery, check:

## Guide Fit

- Current Naver guide basis or assumptions are stated.
- 640 x 640 master and 214 x 214 crop safety are considered when upload-ready.
- Image-type sitelink eligibility is not assumed for insurance without current confirmation.
- No exposure guarantee is implied.

## SERP Fit

- Competitor visuals are summarized as structured observations.
- Saturated motifs are not copied.
- The proposed image uses a clear whitespace or table-stake strategy.
- The image supports the same monthly axis as SA copy and Power Content.

## Product Fit

- The scene matches the insurance product.
- No unrelated product imagery is used.
- No sensational accident, injury, or disaster visual is used.
- If an asset pool is insufficient, new generation is requested instead of forcing a mismatch.

## Style Fit

- Premium 3D animation is preserved.
- The result is not photorealistic, flat icon, childish cartoon, or generic stock-like.
- No text, numbers, logos, badges, or readable UI appear inside the image.
- Four-slot sets vary subject, pose, background, object, and camera framing.
