# Monthly Variation

Monthly image proposals must change meaningfully while keeping a consistent style family.

## Keep Consistent

- premium 3D animation style;
- text-free image rule;
- square composition;
- calm editorial insurance tone;
- product-fit scene library;
- central subject readable at small size.

## Vary Monthly

- scene situation;
- character pose;
- camera angle;
- background room or location;
- main object;
- season or weather only when valid;
- color accent;
- supporting product term.

## Reuse Rule

If a previous source image or concept must be reused as style reference:

- say `style reference only`;
- mark `new generation required`;
- specify what must change: character, pose, background, object, and composition.

Do not present a repeated reference image as a fresh monthly proposal.

## Annual Season Rule

Recurring seasons can repeat, but the exact visual concept should not. For example, Chuseok can rotate among:

- long-distance driver insurance road scene;
- travel insurance airport departure scene;
- home fire insurance vacant-home check scene;
- pet or family preparation only if product scope permits.

Use exact event dates or selected month. Do not use a holiday visual outside its valid month.
