# Naver Image Guide

Use current Naver official guidance when the output will be registered or treated as upload-ready. Naver guide details can change.

## Working Basis

As of the current checked public Naver sources:

- Powerlink image and image-type sitelink guides historically describe square images displayed/cropped at 214 x 214 pixels.
- Naver announced that from 2025-07-16, new registration or "edit then register as new" requires at least 640 x 640 image size for Powerlink image, image sitelink, and medical-specialized image material.
- Image-type sitelinks have had industry restrictions, including finance/insurance restrictions in older guide text. Naver also announced a finance/insurance expansion test excluding lending, so current eligibility should be checked before treating image-type sitelink as upload-ready.

## Practical Production Rule

When no fresher client-specific guide is supplied:

- Create a 640 x 640 square master.
- Check readability and subject framing at a 214 x 214 crop preview.
- Keep key subject large and centered enough for mobile SERP.
- Keep text, numbers, logos, and badges out of the image.
- Export PNG or JPG according to the current upload path.

## Material Types

Consider these separately:

- Powerlink image: representative single image connected to the ad.
- Image-type sitelink: multiple link images, eligibility and exposure may vary by industry and surface.
- Power Content thumbnail: editorial thumbnail that should match the article thesis.
- Body or CTA images: not necessarily Naver SA upload assets, but should share the same visual system.

Do not promise exposure. Naver extension material exposure can vary by surface, performance, and policy.
