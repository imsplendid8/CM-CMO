# Insurance Scene Library

Every thumbnail scene must fit the insurance product. If a matching asset does not exist, request or mark new generation instead of using an unrelated image.

## Product-Fit Scenes

- Driver insurance: intersection, school zone, night driving, accident response, legal document review, safe pull-over, dashboard and road context.
- Home fire insurance: kitchen ceiling leak, balcony storm prep, outlet/small appliance check, fire extinguisher nearby, rain at window, home facility inspection.
- Golf insurance: tee-off, green, golf bag, scorecard, hole-in-one moment, golf course path, clubhouse preparation.
- Hole-in-one insurance: cup and flag, scorecard, golf group celebration, prize certificate implied without readable text.
- Cancer or health insurance: health checkup result, calm consultation room, family discussion, treatment-plan documents, health record folder.
- Dental insurance: dental chair, tooth model, x-ray-like abstract panel without readable labels, treatment consultation, regular checkup.
- Pregnancy/birth insurance: maternity notebook without readable text, ultrasound-like abstract shape, baby room preparation, hospital bag.
- Travel insurance: airport luggage, passport-like booklet without readable text, flight board as blurred shapes without characters, departure gate, lost baggage counter.
- Overseas long stay: campus arrival, dorm room, long-stay luggage, document folder, student desk.
- Event liability: venue safety fence, stage before opening, staff checking flow lines, outdoor event setup.
- Chronic/simple issue insurance: medication organizer without labels, health checklist, blood pressure monitor without readable numbers, consultation desk.

## Avoid By Product

- Do not use baby or pregnancy imagery for cancer insurance.
- Do not use dental imagery for cancer or chronic insurance unless the product is dental.
- Do not use event-staff imagery for golf insurance unless the concept is specifically event liability.
- Do not use generic calculator-only images for every slot.
- Do not use severe accident, injury, hospital emergency, fire explosion, or sensational disaster imagery.

## Visual Tone

Use calm, premium, editorial 3D animation:

- adult Korean characters with natural proportions;
- realistic home, road, clinic, airport, or golf-course spaces;
- soft lighting and clean surfaces;
- clear central subject;
- no childish toy-like look;
- no photorealistic face detail unless requested.
